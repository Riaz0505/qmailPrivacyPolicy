Privacy Policy

Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how Qmail collects, uses, stores and protects information when you use our application.

Last Updated: October 8, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Qmail ("Qmail", "we", "us", or "our"). Qmail is a communication and chat application developed and operated by SCM Global Tech.

Qmail is designed to allow authorized users to communicate, exchange messages and receive application notifications.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, protect and manage information when you use the Qmail mobile application and related services.

By using Qmail, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy.

2. Information We Collect

We collect and process information that is necessary to provide, maintain and secure Qmail's communication services.

2.1 Account and User Information

Depending on how Qmail is configured by the organization using the application, we may process:

  • Name
  • Employee or user identification information
  • Email address or username
  • Account and authentication information
  • Profile information

2.2 Chat and Communication Data

When users use Qmail's communication features, the application may process information voluntarily provided by users, including:

  • Chat messages
  • Messages sent to other users
  • Files or images uploaded through supported communication features
  • Message timestamps
  • Information required to deliver messages

2.3 Device and Technical Information

Qmail may process limited technical information required to operate and secure the application, including where applicable:

  • Device type
  • Operating system information
  • Application version
  • Network and connection information
  • Technical error and diagnostic information

2.4 Push Notification Information

Qmail may use Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) or similar push notification technology to deliver notifications.

A device notification token may be processed for the purpose of delivering notifications related to messages and other application events.

3. How We Use Information

Information processed through Qmail may be used for the following purposes:

  • To create and maintain user accounts.
  • To authenticate authorized users.
  • To provide communication and chat functionality.
  • To send and deliver messages.
  • To deliver push notifications.
  • To maintain application performance.
  • To identify and resolve technical problems.
  • To maintain application security.
  • To prevent unauthorized access, fraud or misuse.

4. Information Sharing

SCM Global Tech does not sell personal information to third parties.

Information may be processed by trusted service providers when necessary to operate Qmail.

These services may include infrastructure, hosting, database, authentication and push notification services.

Such service providers may process information only as necessary to provide their services and in accordance with applicable security and privacy requirements.

5. User Communications

Qmail is a communication and chat application. Messages and other content that users intentionally send through Qmail may be processed and stored so that the intended communication functionality can operate.

Users should avoid sharing highly sensitive personal information through chat unless such sharing is necessary and authorized by their organization.

6. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure or destruction.

Where supported, data transmitted between the application and backend services may be protected using industry-standard encryption technologies such as HTTPS/TLS.

However, no electronic transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. Data Retention

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide Qmail's services, maintain security, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes and enforce applicable agreements.

Chat messages and communication records may be retained according to the data retention requirements of the organization operating or using Qmail.

8. Account and Data Deletion

Users may request deletion of their Qmail account and associated personal information by contacting SCM Global Tech using the contact information provided below.

Upon receiving a valid deletion request, we will process the request in accordance with applicable legal, contractual and organizational requirements.

Certain information may need to be retained where required by law, for legitimate security purposes, or where retention is required by the organization operating the service.

9. Children's Privacy

Qmail is intended for authorized users and is not specifically designed for children.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable laws.

10. Third-Party Services

Qmail may rely on third-party service providers to support certain application functionality.

These services may include hosting, infrastructure, database services, authentication services and push notification services.

Third-party providers may process information as necessary to provide their respective services.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to Qmail, technology, legal requirements or our data practices.

When changes are made, the "Last Updated" date displayed at the beginning of this Privacy Policy will be updated.

12. Contact Us

If you have questions, concerns or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or your personal information, please contact us.
